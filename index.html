Speech Denoising

Making speech easier to hear in noisy recordings

Dayong Fang · Leyu Jiang · Jingyu Liu · Siqi Gong

Project overview

Imagine replaying a lecture or catching up on an online meeting and struggling to hear the speaker over the background. Turning down the noise can also erase quiet sounds that make words distinct. Our project studies how that trade-off changes with the type of noise and the strength of the filter.

We plan to compare a simple frequency filter with two methods that adjust which frequencies to suppress over time. We use paired clean and noisy speech so we can check how much of the original voice each method preserves.

Speech Denoising

Using Time–Frequency Filtering

Dayong Fang · Leyu Jiang · Jingyu Liu · Siqi Gong

Project overview

We study the tradeoff between noise reduction and speech preservation, motivated by noisy lecture recordings and online meetings. We will compare spectral subtraction and Wiener filtering in the short-time Fourier transform (STFT) domain, alongside a conventional FIR baseline and the unprocessed noisy signal.

Our main question is how noise conditions and suppression strength affect the balance between removing noise and preserving speech.
